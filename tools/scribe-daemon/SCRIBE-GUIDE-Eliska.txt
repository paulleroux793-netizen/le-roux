================================================================
  CHAIR-SIDE SCRIBE  -  How to use it   (Surgery 2)
================================================================

WHAT IT DOES
  While you work, the scribe quietly listens and types your spoken
  notes onto the patient's file in Ivory - so you don't have to type.
  You can speak ENGLISH or AFRIKAANS; it understands both.

  Your voice is processed ON THIS PC only. Only the typed text goes
  to Ivory - the audio never leaves the room (POPIA-safe).

----------------------------------------------------------------
IT STARTS BY ITSELF
----------------------------------------------------------------
  * The scribe starts automatically every time you log in to the
    Surgery 2 PC. You do NOT open or click anything to start it.
  * Just make sure the Lark microphone is ON (the clip-on you wear)
    and its little receiver is plugged into the PC.

----------------------------------------------------------------
FOR EACH PATIENT  -  the ONE step that matters
----------------------------------------------------------------
  1. When you seat a patient, open their appointment in the Ivory
     diary and set the status to  "In consultation".

  2. Speak normally. The scribe writes your notes onto THAT patient.

  3. When you finish, change the appointment off "In consultation"
     (e.g. to "Completed"). The scribe stops writing to that patient.

  Why this step?  It tells the scribe which patient is in the chair,
  so notes land on the right file - and it never records anyone who
  hasn't been seated.

----------------------------------------------------------------
GOOD TO KNOW
----------------------------------------------------------------
  * Notes appear a few seconds after you speak (it works in short
    pieces).
  * Speak in full, natural sentences - that transcribes best.
  * The notes are a FIRST DRAFT. Read them on the patient's file and
    tidy / confirm before saving.

----------------------------------------------------------------
IF NO NOTES ARE APPEARING
----------------------------------------------------------------
  1. Is the appointment set to "In consultation"?
  2. Is the Lark mic switched on and the receiver plugged in?
  3. Still nothing? Tell Paul - he can check it in seconds.
