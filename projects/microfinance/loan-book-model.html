Assumptions you must verify before committing capital

The rate-cap formula is contested in public sources. Practitioner commentary splits between (repo × 2.2) + 20% and repo + 21% for unsecured credit — a gap of roughly seven percentage points of gross yield. This model uses the multiplier form and exposes it as an input. Confirm the current schedule against the Government Gazette before pricing a single loan.

The juristic-person exemption is the single largest structural lever here. Under section 4(1) of the Act, a credit agreement falls outside the NCA entirely where the borrower is a juristic person whose asset value or annual turnover meets or exceeds R1 million. That removes rate caps, fee caps, and the prescribed affordability assessment from SME lending to established businesses — which is why the SME model is priced contractually rather than at a ceiling.

Not modelled, and each one is real: the in duplum rule capping accrued interest and fees at the outstanding principal on default; reckless-lending exposure under sections 80–83, which can void an agreement entirely; FICA obligations as an accountable institution; POPIA; mandatory credit bureau reporting; and Debt Collectors Act registration if you collect in-house.

This is a commercial model, not legal advice. The licensing path needs sign-off from a credit-regulatory attorney before a rand goes out the door.
